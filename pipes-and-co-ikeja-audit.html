2. The gap, stated plainly

Someone with a burst pipe or a bathroom job searches on their phone. They find your listing, see a name, an address and a number, and they have to decide in seconds whether to call you or the next plumber on the list. Nothing on your listing tells them what you stock or what work you take. The decision becomes a guess, and a guess usually goes to whoever looks more established.

The one-line version: your listing gets you onto the shortlist and gives the customer nothing to choose you on. One link changes that.

3. First three fixes, in order

Fix 1 — Put a link on the listing

Open the listing in Google Maps, choose "Add website", and paste a link. A one-page list of the materials you stock and the jobs you take is enough. About ten minutes.

Fix 2 — Add photos of stock and completed work

Upload your shelves, the counter, and a few finished jobs. Plumbing and building materials are bought on visible proof that you have the stock. You can do this from your phone.

Fix 3 — Ask customers for reviews

The listing has a "Write a review" button. Ask customers whose jobs you finished to leave an honest review. Reviews are one of the signals Google uses to decide who appears first in local search, and they compound.
