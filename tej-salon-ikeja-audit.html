2. The gap, stated plainly

Most of the work is already done. You have claimed the listing, a contact number is on it, and your hours are correct. Plenty of salons in Ikeja have not managed that.

What is missing is the link. When a customer in Allen searches "hair salon near me" and taps your listing, they get your name and a number to call, and nothing else. No prices, no list of services, no photos of your work, no way to judge whether you do what they want before they ring. The customer choosing between three salons on that same screen has nothing to look at from you.

The one-line version: your listing gets the customer to the phone call, but it gives them no reason to prefer you over the salon listed underneath. One link fixes that, and it takes about 20 minutes.

3. First three fixes, in order

Fix 1 — Put a link on the listing

Open the listing in Google Maps, choose "Add website", and paste a link. If you have no website, your Instagram profile or a free one-page site works. This is the single highest-value change: it turns a name-and-number listing into something a customer can actually evaluate. Roughly 10 minutes.

Fix 2 — Add photos of your work

Upload finished styles, the space, the team, and the entrance so people recognise it from the road. Salons are chosen on visuals. You can do this from your phone between clients.

Fix 3 — Ask your regulars for a review

The listing has a "Write a review" button. Ask ten regulars this week for an honest review.
