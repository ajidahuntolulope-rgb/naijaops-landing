2. The gap, stated plainly

A patient in Agege looking for a dentist finds your listing, sees that you are open and that you have a number, and taps. That is where it stops. There is no link, so there is nothing to read about what you treat, what a visit costs, or how to book without calling. A dental decision is made slowly and with reading. Your listing does not offer any reading.

The one-line version: your listing proves you exist and gives a phone number. It gives the patient no way to learn anything else. One link changes that.

3. First three fixes, in order

Fix 1 — Put a link on the listing

Open the listing in Google Maps, choose "Add website", and paste a link. A simple page listing your services, hours and how to book is enough. About ten minutes.

Fix 2 — Add photos of the clinic

Upload the reception, the treatment room and the entrance so a nervous patient knows what they are walking into. Dental visits are anxiety purchases. Familiarity reduces that. You can do this from your phone.

Fix 3 — Ask patients for reviews

The listing has a "Write a review" button. Ask patients you have treated recently for an honest review. Review volume is one of the signals Google uses to decide who appears first in local search, and it compounds.
