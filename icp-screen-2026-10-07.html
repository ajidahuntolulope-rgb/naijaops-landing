What the 8 actually show

The non-restaurant pass confirms the thesis and sharpens it. Seven of eight are reachable

by phone or WhatsApp. Only two are unclaimed. But **seven of eight have no website on their

listing** — that, not "unclaimed", is the dominant gap in these categories.

That changes the pitch. For Cafecito the hook is "your listing looks abandoned". For a salon,

clinic, school or spare-parts shop in Ikeja the hook is narrower and more concrete:

**"you have no link on your Google listing, so the customer who finds you has no way to see

your services, prices or work."**

The other finding: most visible Ikeja businesses are already sorted. 56 of 86 screened had

phone + website. Roughly two-thirds. Approaching them wastes a message. Tier 1 and Tier 2 are

the only lists worth the first 20 DMs.

Caveats, stated plainly

• Photo counts are not exposed by the public Maps view. What is recorded is Google's own

"Add photos & videos" prompt, which appears when a listing has little or no owner media.

That is a proxy, not a count. Do not quote a photo number to a prospect.

• "—" in the rating column means the listing did not display a rating in the view captured.

It does not mean zero.

• Listings change. Re-check a row immediately before sending to that business.

• No third-party data is used in this file. Everything came from the Google Maps listing

itself. Where a phone number appears, it is the number Google displays.
