## Why it is written this way

- **Line 1 proves a human looked.** Names the street. Not a template.
- **Line 2 is the whole pitch in one sentence**: they are strong on Instagram and invisible on
  Google. It flatters and accuses at once, and every clause is checkable on their own listing.
- **Line 3 offers the artifact**, not a service. An audit is easier to accept than a proposal.
- **Line 4 removes risk**: no charge, nothing to sign.
- **No price in the first message.** The ₦15,000 offer sits in the audit PDF, which they only
  see after they say yes. Leading with price turns a conversation into a transaction.

the public listing."* That invites a correction, which keeps them talking.
