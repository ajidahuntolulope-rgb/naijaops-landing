# NaijaOps — Ikeja Market Screen, 7 October 2026

A survey of how Ikeja businesses appear on Google Maps, and which ones have a gap worth fixing.
Business names are listed. **Contact details are not published here.**

## What was checked

86 unique Ikeja-area businesses were opened on their live Google Maps listing on 7 October 2026.
For each one we recorded what a visitor sees: whether the listing is claimed, whether a phone
number is displayed, whether a website link is present, and whether Google is showing an
"Add photos & videos" prompt.

## What we found

| Finding | Count |
|---|---|
| Businesses checked | 86 |
| Dropped: sponsored/duplicate scrape rows | 3 |
| Dropped: government facilities and public health centres | 5 |
| Dropped: large institutions with in-house marketing | 3 |
| Dropped: markets (not a single buyer) | 1 |
| Dropped: outside Ikeja | 13 |
| **Ikeja private businesses in the final screen** | **61** |
| — of which unclaimed | 4 |
| — of which claimed but missing a phone or website | 15 |
| — of which already complete (phone + website) | 42 |

## The headline

**The most common gap is not an unclaimed listing. It is a missing link.**

Of the businesses we examined, the large majority have already claimed their Google listing and
put a phone number on it. What they have not done is give the customer anything to read: no
website, no services, no prices, no way to judge the business before calling. That is a small,
fixable gap — and it is the one we work on.

Roughly two-thirds of the businesses we looked at are already complete. We say so plainly rather
than pretending every listing is broken.

## What we do about it

Google Business Profile setup from ₦15,000: claim or verify the listing, add the link, add
photos, and prompt reviews — done with the owner, in one sitting.
